# Progress Diary

> Keep a coherent diary of progress toward what matters.

**Status**: 🟡 Designed · Not yet implemented (v1.2 roadmap)
**Skills**: (to be defined)

## For whom

Anyone who wants to see their trajectory across days, weeks, and months — not just today. Founders tracking momentum. Parents tracking family rhythms. Learners tracking what they've actually picked up.

Today, daily notes capture *what happened*. They don't easily surface *what advanced* or *what got stuck*. Progress Diary fills that gap.

## What it does (planned)

- **End of day**: Virgil drafts a short diary entry — today's wins, what advanced, what got blocked
- **You review** — accept, edit, or skip. Low friction by design
- **Weekly**: Virgil prompts a longer reflection synthesising the week's diary entries
- **Monthly**: Virgil surfaces patterns and trajectory across recent weeks
- All entries link to goals (if a Goals database is configured), so you see progress *in the context of what you're aiming at*

## Outcome

You can look back and see your actual progress, not just your activity. The diary becomes the memory layer between daily notes (raw) and goals (aspirational).

## Status
